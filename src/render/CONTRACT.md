# render: drawing and feel

**Owns:** backdrop, stage art, standees (pose, lean, squash, flash, tint), cutout rigs (part layout, joint poses, blends, held weapons in hand slots), props and floor items, effects (incl. debris), screen shake + hit-stop counters, HUD, debug overlay.
**Does not own:** gameplay state. Render reads `world` and listens to bus events; it never writes to fighters or world.

**Public API:** `createRenderer(ctx, assets)`, `createJuice()`, `drawStandee`, `drawRig`, `rigFrame`, `rigMemory`, `lastFrame` (T027 viewer), `floorLayer`, `socketOf`, `socketPoints`, `braceLayer`, `layoutRig`, `heldOf`, `restIn`, `handleAngle`, `gripLayout`, `gearLayout`, `tintPixels`, `drawHud`, `drawDebug`, `stepViewPose`, `squatOf`, `feelLayer`, T028: `applyViews`, `viewLayer`, `viewRig`, `springLayer`, `carryLayer`, `carryMotion`.

**Rules:**
- Look numbers live in `src/data/juice.js`, move `pose`/`fx` blocks in `src/data/moves/` and rig parts/poses in `src/data/rigs/`.
- Render-only memory (rig pose blends) is kept in render, keyed by fighter, never written onto the fighter.
- Render-only randomness (sparks, shake) may use `Math.random`.
- Draw art from data paths only; a missing image shows a labeled placeholder.
- Part swaps (`swap` in rig poses) only change which image a part draws; pivots and attach points stay in the part's own pixels. A swap may also hide other parts and carry children onto its own image (`hide`, `carry`; T023 `turn`: the carried item turned onto that fist's slot axis): still drawing only, no bones or gameplay change. T023: an empty hand slot (sheathed, thrown, broken) puts on the rig's `emptyHand` drawings unless the pose names that part.
- Held weapons are generic: a kit's `grip` names a rig slot (`rig.slots`), the kit gives image, grip point, rest angle and `px` size; the weapon follows the slot part's joint. New grip types (two-hand, bow: T011) = a new slot + `rig.grips` entry + kit data; `rig-grip.js` bends the second arm by IK onto the kit's point (spear shaft, bow string) and draws the bow string and nocked arrow. T020: a rig's `axes[grip]` (the fist's slot axis) and the item's `gripEnd` (handle axis) turn the item so its handle runs through the fist (`restIn`); a grip may `align` the IK fist round the shaft, change draw `order` while gripping, and draw a bow to an `anchor` on the body (the ear). A fighter's `kit` (or look-only `art.held`, T018) is drawn the same way; once a KO'd fighter has dropped that item his hand is empty. T025: the weapon is also a node of its own (`weapon-node.js`): a pose may place it (`weapon: { x, y, rot }`) and name grip modes (`grip`, `grip2`: side / overhand / underhand / reverse, `data/grip-modes.js`); the grip hand then copies the weapon's rotation and the arms reach it, never the other way round; a pose that does neither draws exactly as before. Grip hands never draw under their weapon. Debris pieces live only in render memory. T028 r5: a grip's `along` ([x, y] in the item's px) is the line its second hand aligns to instead of the handle (the bow's string hand: the arrow's line).
- T028 r4: a rig with the user's grip lines (`rig.gripLines`, data/rigs/grip-lines.js) holds every item by one rule: the item's grip point on the hand's grip centre, its handle direction along the hand's line (`rig.itemLines` override the kit's own points for that rig only, `linedKit`); grip modes without their own hand drawing fall back to side; a second fist out of reach of the shaft lets go. A rig without the lines holds exactly as before.
- Scrolling stages (T013, `layers.js`): far/back/floor/back decor drawn before the world, front decor after it; the world layer is shifted by `-round(w.cam.x)`. The HUD and debug get `camX` for anything placed at world positions.
- `opts.poses[slot]` (C key, slot 0) shows a named rig pose on that hero for viewing (once, from idle; `view-pose.js`); it never touches the fighter.
- Bracing loop (T015, `brace.js`): a rig with `brace` data breathes, sways and twitches while blocking (not in blockstun); legs are re-solved so the boots never leave their spot. Render memory only.
- Weapon feel (T017, `weapon-feel.js` + rig `kits` poses): a held floor weapon may have its own idle/walk and move poses on a rig (`poses.kits[<item id>]`, falling back to the borrowed sword pose) and a kit `feel` (wobble spring, impact squash); moves may carry view-only `fx.dust`, `fx.shake`, `fx.drops`. None of these change sim numbers.
- Heroes (T021): HUD draws one panel per hero (slot x 320 px along the top); heroes on slots 1, 2 are drawn with `JUICE.heroes.filter[slot]` (a canvas filter: the test hero is a tinted Mouse Fighter) and tagged P1-P3 when there is more than one.
- Gear and looks (T019, `looks.js`): gear overlays (a fighter's `art.gear` loadout + its `look.detail`) ride their part, picking the variant for the part's current swap from `rig.views`; cloth/fur masks (`rig.masks`) recolour images per look, cached per image + look (never per frame); `look.size` scales the drawn rig only. Render reads `f.look`, never writes it.
- Helping (T022, `helping.js`): downed timer ring, revive ring and prompt, potion in hand are drawn from sim state (it may call the pure helpers in `sim/revive.js`); revive and heal bursts come from the `revive`/`heal` events (`prop-fx.js`). Numbers in `JUICE.help`.
- T024 names: limbs, hands and feet are `near` / `far` (`arm-near-upper`, `hand-far`, `foot-near`...; `head-back`, `torso-back` = the back view). The importer and bench read old front/back sources.
- T024 floor-line rule (`floor.js`): in grounded states each boot's drawing sits on its own floor line (master contact height; the far boot's is higher in 3/4), raised only by the pose's `'<foot>.lift'` channel (blends like any joint); the foot's tilt is the pose's, the IK only moves the ankle up/down. The standee's lean turns the hips instead of tipping the figure round its feet. Whole-leg drawings (kneeling, folded) turn at the hip onto the line. Lying states rest the body's lowest point on the floor. A rig with a `downed` pose kneels (no standee lying tilt). Render only.
- T024 sockets (`sockets.js`): every rig declares `sockets` (`hand_near`, `hand_far`, `weapon_tip`, `chest`, `head_top`, `feet`, `mouth`, `tail_tip`; importer-derived); `drawRig` keeps the last drawn world points; effects use `socketOf(f, name)` and fall back to their own numbers for standees (fish drops at the weapon tip, heal ring at the chest, potion in the near fist / at the mouth).
- T024 phase swaps: a cycle pose's `swapPhase: [{ part, swap, from, to }]` shows a drawing in that window of the cycle (the walk's toe-off feet).
- T026: a rig may name `heldHand` (the drawing a hand holding its item puts on: A018's default hands are fists); a grip
  mode's own hand drawing (`<hand>-overhand`) is placed at its own slot (`rig.hands`). Old rigs (no such fields or
  drawings) draw exactly as before.
- T028 views (`rig-views.js`): a rig's `bodyViews` give the torso and pelvis views (drawing, joint points, draw bands); a pose names them (`view`), and per-part bands (`order`), both switching at once like swaps; the children slide to the new points over `RIG_MOTION.viewBlend`. Shoulder caps show only while their arm draws in front. The turn-around through F when the facing flips, tail and scarf springs (`springs.js`) and the carry socket (`carry.js`) are render memory only. A rig without `bodyViews` / `springs` / `carry` lays out exactly as before (tests/views.test.mjs). T028 r2: a view may also re-hang limbs (`attach`, the back view's limb cuts) and hide parts (`hide`: B's belt); split grips (`grip-split.js`): a grip drawing's `under` piece draws just under the held item with the hand's matrix, as an extra draw-list entry `<hand>:under`; a move may name a `recover` key (passed mid-recovery).
