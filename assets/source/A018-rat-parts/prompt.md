# A018 prompts and preparation

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen` (imagegen skill), edit mode with referenced local images, `transparent_background=false`. Model/seed/quality controls were not exposed. All raw tool images remain outside this delivery; only final requested cuts and swaps are included.

Inputs: A017 `master.png` and `master-back.png`; A010 `head-neutral`, `attack`, `hurt`, `effort`, `ko`, `back`. Style reference inspected: `docs/concepts/art-bible/contact-sheet.jpg`.

The exact prompts below are in call order. `jointPrompt` used a 2048×2560, four-column, five-row sheet of native master cuts, plus the master. `handPrompt` used a 2048×1024 five-column/two-row sheet repeating the cut near/far fists. `sidePrompt` used the native torso cut enlarged for the tool. `backPrompt` used a crop of the rear chest and the rear master. `capPrompt` edited the first joint-sheet result. `diskPrompt` used the approved master as material reference.

The first joint sheet and its revision redrew visible parts, so they are discarded rather than delivered as body art. The material disks from the final call are cut out, uniformly resized, and composited beneath native cut pixels at measured joints. Where an overlapping disk would cover another visible master region, its registered master pixels are restored; this preserves the resting outfit but can carry adjacent garment texture into bends (see HANDOFF). No source body part or head is stretched. Disk paint is image-tool output, not procedural painting.

Heads: paste the A010 source RGB onto a 1536×2304 #00FF00 canvas at dx=-114, dy=-50, without interpolation. All six output canvases independently compared pixel-for-pixel to this operation, including green fringe. Head-neutral is delivered as `head.png`.

Feet: cut from the master, then translate near by (0,-1), far by (0,+26), inclusive sole y=1950. Their pivots translate with them. No warp or limb-length change.

Hand alternatives: isolated tool drawings uniformly scaled by 0.70 near / 0.69 far and positioned at the shared wrist pivots; native fists are retained as defaults. Torso-side is uniformly fitted to 410 px width; torso-back to 550 px width, both registered at the default waist pivot. See HANDOFF for the repaint limitation.

Review: Pillow used only for slicing, green masking, translation, uniform scaling, layer compositing and labels. Review shows source-scale assembly over a 30% master ghost, all parts/swaps on grey, every hand in place and a 136 px rat over Harbor Market. Source cuts are all 1536×2304 RGB on exact green.
