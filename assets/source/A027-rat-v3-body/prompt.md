## Measurement and sweep method

Foreground is non-green. Whole-rat IoU compares final union to the keyed master, counting extras and misses. Per-piece IoU compares final exposed part masks, independently occluded in manifest draw order, to manual master material ownership traces. Traced interstices were completed by nearest region before seeing generated paint; these are not independent ground-truth segmentations, and owning a different boundary can lower a score without a large whole-rat change. Original master paint restoration deliberately strengthens the rest match; this metric does not prove hidden surfaces are finished. Each sweep rigidly rotates the selected child and all descendants around its exact master pivot. Five evenly spaced angles, positive clockwise; local source-pixel overlap and 136px foreground-height rendering. Nonzero overlap alone does not pass the no-gap/no-double-outline requirement.
