Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, two local image edits, opaque green background. No CLI fallback, no seed exposed.

## Torso edit (exact prompt)

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: two-cell production cutout atlas, left ordinary rat torso, right same torso wearing brown leather jerkin. Keep exact 1280x580 atlas canvas, same two 640x580 cells, exact scale and positions. Remove the LARGE near shoulder armor pauldron in the UPPER LEFT of EACH torso (silver metal dome, orange leather padded rim, round gold button, and furry jagged flap below it). This entire shoulder armor will be moved to a separate arm asset. In its place paint a smooth completed chest/vest armhole: left warm ochre cloth/leather vest with finished dark outlined curved outer edge and warm rat fur lining; right continuous dark brown leather jerkin with finished dark outlined curved armhole. Fill the former missing jagged shoulder region enough for an arm to swing freely over it. The torso must remain a broad connected chest, not a hole. No metal shoulder plate and no gold shoulder button left on either torso. Remove the jagged fur spikes at that left shoulder cut only, replacing with a clean finished curved vest boundary. Preserve EXACTLY all other pixels and designs: red bandana, neck fur and neck opening, diagonal chest strap and buckle, belly fur on left, entire jerkin and belts on right, far shoulder at right. Bold dark outlines, warm painted cartoon shading, light upper left. Pure flat #00FF00 green background. No new parts, labels, shadows, limbs or scenery.

## Arm edit (exact prompt)

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit this existing production rat upper-arm cutout. Keep exact 500x530 canvas and exact arm/bracelet placement, all lower arm fur and cuff paint unchanged. The metal-and-orange-leather shoulder pad at the upper right is now part of this arm. Precisely reshape ONLY its UPPER OUTER edge into a smooth ROUND ARC centered at pixel (283,189.5), radius 139 pixels. Thus arc top is (283,50.5), left point (144,189.5), right point (422,189.5). The outline should follow that circular arc across the upper half, and blend naturally into the existing leather lower rim and arm below. Fill the expanded pad top with the SAME silver metal cap, orange leather, gold stitching; retain existing pad interior paint, rivet and lower rim as much as possible. Finish a thick smooth dark outline along the round edge. The shoulder rotation point is the center of the arc, NOT its top. The circular top should give an invariant silhouette when this cutout rotates. Do not add a separate ball or fur disc: this is a rounded armor shoulder. Remove little green slivers and stray red scarf scraps on the metal cap. Keep unchanged all fur and bracer below y=300. Pure flat #00FF00 background, no pivot dot, no text, no shadow, no new parts. Same warm painted cartoon style with bold dark outline.

## Registration and preservation

Torso input: two 640×580 source crops at [420,960,1060,1540], ordinary A023 torso and A020 torso-jerkin. Donor normalized uniformly from 1863×844 to the input atlas dimensions, retained only in the recorded shoulder repair polygon. All original pixels outside that polygon retained. Generated green normalized to #00FF00.

Arm input: A024 upper arm with the original A023 torso pauldron selected and translated [41,1] into the child's canvas space. Crop [350,900,850,1430]. Generated donor normalized uniformly from 1218×1291 to 500×530. Its upper arc was fitted to a circle and uniformly registered to radius 139 source pixels, center [633,1089.5]. Only the shoulder pad donor was retained; existing arm pixels outside its alpha support were restored exactly. The same pad was oriented toward each whole-arm key's elbow and translated to that key's unchanged [555,1174] shoulder pivot; no arm, fist, grip or bracer was rescaled or regenerated. Geometry and donor fit are in self-check.json. Review composition uses Pillow with the project's green key formula; no generated review variants or raw atlases are delivered.


The upper-arm user point is converted from the original A023 trimmed drawing. Whole-arm keys retain their individual source shoulder pivots. Detached donor flecks were removed after compositing; the preservation audit reports those removals explicitly.
