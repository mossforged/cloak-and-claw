## What I would pick

**A for the first playable home.** Its horizontal stone bays communicate individual repairs without making navigation depend on stairs or tree platforms. The hill gives shelter and the low defensive gate gives the stronghold a clear silhouette. Each rescue can change one bay while the shared wall stays recognizable.

**B for the strongest landmark.** The huge tree feels most distinctive and warm. Keep its roots behind the floor and reserve the canopy for a background layer if selected; otherwise it can visually crowd service entrances.

**C for ambient motion.** A repaired wheel, running water, chimney smoke and roof birds could make coming home feel alive. It would need care to keep water behind the walking strip and the wheel from dominating the small services.

Keep the rumor board, thimble-capped lantern, communal fire, blue/red cloth rack and all four harmless critters. The forge flame, kitchen pot, healer herbs/cot and tailor cloth rack give the strongest service cues. The watch post and garden need larger silhouette changes at game size.

## Tool limits and review notes

- Pixel coordinates in prompts are approximate guidance to the generator. The home files are exactly 960 × 540, but the painted belt boundaries and hero height are not measured game geometry. Later layer packets must enforce the 120 px floor depth and use the actual 130 px hero sprite.
- A targeted geometry pass improved the open floor and introduced mixed rebuilding states in all three frames, but the hero remains undersized, especially in B. B also lost its title during that edit; its six service labels and mission exit remain visible, and the filename identifies the direction.
- The exterior direction views use more fine texture and softer outlines than the game-frame mockups. Use the home frames for the closer style target; simplify masonry further for final art.
- The before/after sheet keeps the service order and overall scene, but masonry and roof details drift. Its paired panels are wide comparison thumbnails, not separately exported 960 × 540 frames.
- Some generic fortified architecture, decorative heraldry and detailed props were invented by the generator; these are suggestions, not approved lore or designs. The figures in the payoff sheet are generic grey silhouettes, not character concepts.
- No game-ready layers, final characters, UI or code are included. Nothing is missing from the requested sheet set. Direction and individual pieces still require human selection.
