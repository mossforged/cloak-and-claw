## Round 3 (lead, 2026-10-19): wrists have limits
The user's look at round 2 (phone): "most of the others are ok", but on the staff "this is just not how limbs connect", and on the spear-style lunge "the base of the hand is disconnected from the end of the forearm". He asked whether Code knows the grip line but not the wrist. **He's right.** Lead measurement on the branch (hand angle relative to its forearm, per frame): the staff swing turns the near hand up to ~110 deg and the far hand ~170 deg away from their rest angle on the forearm. The grip solver (rig-grip.js `alignGrip`, weapon-node.js) turns the fist about the wrist until it lies along the shaft, with no limit, and nothing ever checks the wrist. The grip-line test checks hand-to-item; nothing checks hand-to-forearm. A real wrist bends about 45-70 deg; past that the forearm and elbow turn instead, or the hold changes.
1. **Wrist limits as data** (`src/data/`, per rig, per hand): how far the hand may turn from its drawn rest angle on the forearm (start: +-45 deg, both hands; lab slider). Keep the wrist joint itself closed: the hand's pivot on the forearm's wrist point.
2. **Solve the arm to the hand's angle, not just its position.** When a grip needs the hand turned past the limit, the forearm (and the upper arm through IK) turns so the wrist stays inside it: the IK target is the wrist point *and* a forearm direction within the limit of the hand's axis. If no arm pose reaches, the item turns (the node's rot gives toward what the arms can do), never the wrist. A two-hander places the item so both hands are inside their limits (line through both grips).
3. **The far raised arm (rigid whole-arm drawing) holds the staff only if the staff goes through its fist**; otherwise the staff overhead uses the jointed far arm (upper + forearm + fist) with IK. No empty raised fist beside a held staff.
4. **Test:** every hand, every frame of every clip and rehearsal key: wrist bend within its limit and the hand's pivot on the forearm's wrist point (0.5 px). It fails with the clip, frame, hand and angle. No known-list for this one.
5. Redo the poses this changes (staff swing and overhead, spear thrust, any slash or chop the test catches), rerun the pass bar, refresh views.json and the sheet. Report the before/after worst wrist bend per clip.
