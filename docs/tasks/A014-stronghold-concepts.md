# A014 - The stronghold: broad concept sheets (Art)

**Read:** `docs/handoffs/game-brief.md` (the hook), `docs/design/home-loop.md` (what home is for), `docs/style-contract.md`, `docs/concepts/art-bible/contact-sheet.jpg` (the style), `assets/art/stages/harbor-market.png` and `assets/source/A007-harbor-stage-kit/review.png` (how a stage frame looks at game scale).
**Autonomy:** human-gated (the user picks a direction and the pieces he likes). **Needs:** nothing.
**Deliver to:** `docs/concepts/A014-stronghold/` (this packet's only output folder; concept sheets are reference, not game art). **JPG, quality ~85, each sheet under 1 MB, whole folder under 10 MB.** No PNG, nothing in `assets/source/`, no raw tool outputs.

Between missions the hero comes home to a ruined stronghold that the folk he rescues move into and rebuild. Every rescue should change the place **visibly** (a ruined spot becomes a working one, with its new resident standing by it). Stage 5 needs a grey-box version of this home and Stage 6 the real one, so before anything is built we want a **broad look** at what the place could be. This is exploration: quantity and range over polish. Later art tasks turn the picked direction into game-ready layers.

Call it "the stronghold" everywhere (no names, no lore). It is an abandoned, **abbey-like** place but **not an abbey**: avoid cloisters, bell towers and monks as the main read. Keep it grounded and natural, built at animal scale from stone, timber, roots, clay and found human-world objects where they fit (Everdell's grounded materials, not fanciful magic). Warm, heroic, hopeful even in ruin; never grim.

## Sheets

Suggestions, swap any for something better: (A) a stone keep dug into a hillside with burrow rooms; (B) old stone ruins grown through by a huge hollow tree; (C) a riverside fort around a broken water mill. Label A/B/C.
2. `02-home-frame-A.jpg`, `-B.jpg`, `-C.jpg`: for each direction, a **mock 960 x 540 game frame** of the one walkable room or courtyard, in the side-on belt view our stages use (floor about **120 px deep**, back wall behind it, a foreground piece or two). Mouse Fighter (about 130 px, from the art bible) standing on the floor for scale. Show where **4 to 6 building spots** sit along the back wall, plus a way out to missions (a gate, door or path) at one side.
3. `03-building-states.jpg` (may be split into two sheets): **six building spots, each in three states**: ruined, cleared (rubble gone, scaffold up), restored and in use. Spots: smithy / forge, kitchen and hearth, healer's room, workshop (tailor), watch post, storehouse or garden. Restored versions show what they do at a glance (anvil glow, steam from a pot, herbs drying, cloth on a rack). Leave a clear standing spot in front of each for its future resident.
4. `04-props-and-life.jpg`: a grab bag of pieces: rubble piles, broken beams, scaffolds, banners and bunting, lanterns, a central fire, a **notice / rumor board** (where skipped rescues and missions are pinned), a supply cart, crates and sacks. Plus **ambient life** that never looks like an enemy: a beetle trundling a pebble, a snail on the wall, birds on the roof, moths at a lantern.
5. `05-before-after.jpg`: one direction of your choice shown twice in the same frame: day one (empty ruin) and a full stronghold (every spot restored, bunting, lights, smoke), so we can judge the payoff.
6. `prompt.md` (tool + exact prompts) and `HANDOFF.md` (what you'd pick and why, what the tool struggled with).

## Rules
- Art-bible style: warm painted cartoon, bold dark outlines, light from the upper left, palette of warm browns, muted reds, blues, golds.
- Stage pieces are **side-on with a slight top-down tilt**, like Harbor Market, so they could later become layers (back wall, floor strip, front props).
- Background colour doesn't matter here (these are sheets, not cut-outs); labels in plain text on the sheet are welcome.
- No new characters: residents are shown only as simple grey silhouettes or not at all (characters go through the rig standard later).

## Acceptance
- All sheets present, JPG, size limits met, labelled.
- Human: which direction feels like home worth rebuilding? Which building states read clearly as before/after at game size? Which props and critters to keep?

Out of scope: game-ready layers, characters, UI, code.
