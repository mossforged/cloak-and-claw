## What changed
1. **Wrist limits as data** (`src/data/wrists.js`, with a "Wrists" section in the lab). The limit is ±45° per hand, and a rig can override it.
   - Each hand drawing also has a "natural angle" on the forearm. It is 0 (as drawn) for every drawing except the near overhand fist and the far bow grip, which are +90.
   - The reason: a fist closes round a handle across the palm, so the grip line should run across the forearm. Those two drawings have their grip line running *along* the hanging forearm.
   - Measured from as drawn, the user-approved spearman idle and archer stance would already be 120–138° bent, so without this they would have been wrongly flagged.
2. **The arm is solved to the hand's angle** (`src/render/wrist.js`, run at the end of the grip layout).
   - When a hand holding something is past its limit, the forearm is turned to a direction inside the limit and the upper arm reaches the elbow that leaves.
   - Where the arms can't reach, the item turns or moves; the wrist never passes its limit. The choice goes by cost (degrees the item turns against pixels the hand moves), and all the costs are in data.
   - On a two-hander, the second hand is solved with the item: it may slide back along the shaft but never off it. If it can't stay on the shaft, it lets go.
   - Empty hands simply turn back to the limit.
3. **The archer's string hand** now draws with the elbow pulled back level with the arrow and a straight wrist. Before, the wrist was bent back 135°; the bow stays upright and the string stays at the jaw.
4. **Staff overhead (item 3):** the wind-up now holds the staff with the jointed far arm (upper arm, forearm, overhand fist on the second grip). The rigid raised-arm drawing ended up as an empty fist beside the shaft once the staff turned. The wrist test now fails if any whole-arm drawing sits more than 2 px off a two-handed item it doesn't hold.
5. **Wrist test** (`tests/wrist.test.mjs`, no known list):
   - It checks every hand in every frame of every clip and rehearsal key: inside the limit and on the wrist point (0.5 px).
   - A failure names the fighter, clip, frame, hand and angle.
6. **Poses redone so the moves don't lean on the solver:**
   - Spearman idle: the spear sits 4 px back and 8 px lower.
   - Spear thrust: all three keys re-placed. The strike sits 8 px back and 10 px lower than before.
   - Staff swing: the wind-up keeps its angle and sits 12 px back; the strike is now level (it was angled 14° down).
   - Staff overhead wind-up: moved 14 px back and 14 px up.
   - The raider's one-handed swings were left to the solver: before/after renders show they mostly keep their read.
