## Why the feet were off
I traced the worst frame of each move, foot by foot. All three moves use keys marked `aloft` (off the floor), and while one of those keys shows, the game skips the floor rule. But the rat never leaves the ground in these moves: the hop is posed with the body raised, not a real jump.

- **Dodge hop (frames 8 and 29) and jump attack (frame 46):** the aloft key switches on at once, but its height blends in over a few frames. On take-off and landing the body is still crouched at floor height with no floor rule, so the feet sink:
  - hop: the far foot is 22.8 px below its line;
  - jump attack: 23.4 px below.

  The next few frames also sank the raised feet 4–23 px below the floor. The old table didn't count those, because a raised foot isn't "planted".
- **Kick:** its wind-up and strike keys are aloft so the kicking leg stays up. That also left the standing far toe as posed, 10–11 px above its line for 34 frames.
- No joint showed at a limit because the limit solve never ran on these frames.

## The fix
This only applies to a rig with joint limits (v3), so the old rat is untouched. On a frame where an aloft key shows but the rat is on the ground:
- A planted foot (raised less than 0.5 px) goes onto its line with the same limit-aware solve T035 added. The 0.5 px setting is in `src/data/limit-solve.js` as `planted`.
- A raised foot that would sink below the floor stops on it.
- Any other foot stays as posed.
