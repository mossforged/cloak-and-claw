## What the user gets
- **Opening it:** `viewer.html?pose=<fighter>&from=<clip>:<frame>` (default the idle, optional `&item=`). In the normal viewer, the View tab has a new **"Pose this frame"** button that opens the page on the frame showing.
- **Handles:** the rig at full phone width, with round handles on each hand and foot (white), the hips (blue) and the head (green). Each hand and foot also has a small twist ring. Touch and mouse both work.
- **Drag a hand or foot:** the arm or leg follows by two-bone IK, using the game's own IK function.
  - The elbow or knee keeps the side it bends to, and never folds deeper than its limit (or deeper than the starting pose already had it).
  - The wrist stays inside the T029 limits. Ankles stay inside a new limit, set to 60°.
  - Out of reach, the limb stops at full stretch pointing at the finger and never comes off.
  - A raised foot keeps its height above the floor.
- **Drag the hips:** the body moves and the feet stay where they stand. If a leg can't keep its foot, the hips stop.
- **Drag the head:** it turns on the neck, up to 40°.
- **Twist ring:** turns the hand or foot up to its limit, then the forearm or shin turns the rest, as the game's solver does.
- **Weapons:** a held item stays in the fist.
  - On a two-handed weapon (the spearman's), the first fist is gold: dragging it moves the item, and its ring turns it. Where the wrist limits would hold the fist back, the item also turns, and the second fist stays on the shaft.
  - The second fist is a hollow gold ring: dragging it swings the item round the first fist.
- **Panel:**
  - Buttons: **Copy pose** (the pose as the data line a move file takes), **Share** (a link that holds the whole pose), Undo, Reset.
  - Pickers: start clip and frame; view (Q, S, F, BQ, B); a drawing picker for each hand and foot.
  - Joint readout for every joint, red when at its limit.
- **Same draw path as the game:** the pose is drawn with the game's own drawing code, and a test checks the start pose matches the game's frame within 0.5 px.
